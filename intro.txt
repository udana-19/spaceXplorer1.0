=== SPACEXPLORER ===

You are an astronaut stranded in the depths of space.
Your mission is to navigate through the void, collect valuable space junk,
and find your way back home.

Watch out for the asteroid! If it hits you, your mission is over.
Space junk can be used to repair your ship and refuel.

Legend:
P - Player (you)
A - Asteroid (avoid!)
* - Space Junk (collect)
H - Home (reach to win)

Good luck, explorer!
